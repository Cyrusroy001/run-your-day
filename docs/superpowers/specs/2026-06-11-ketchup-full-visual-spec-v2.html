The day planner that catches you up.

When you fall behind, ketchup quietly squeezes the flexible parts of your day and protects the locked ones. No red alarms — tomato is the brand, mustard is the only caution, leaf means done. One sentence tells you what changed.

The tilde ~ survives the rebrand: it's the "approximately" mark on every -ish time ("8-ish"), the squeeze glyph — and, drawn thick, it's literally a drizzle of ketchup. That squiggle is the app icon.

01

Design rules — the why

Every screen obeys these. The ban list comes straight from the v1 build's screenshots — each item shipped once and must never ship again.

R1One surface for today

Home and Live are the same screen. The hero is the timeline's first stop, enlarged. The v1 build rendered the drift summary twice (Home card + Live banner) — merging the surfaces makes duplication impossible.

R2One sentence on screen

The whisper names the latest squeeze and the protected anchor — never the full list. The v1 four-line paragraph enumerating five trimmed blocks becomes: "Gym ran long, so I squeezed brunch. Work is untouched." The rail shows the rest visually.

R3Condiment colors only

char/raise (ground), salt/dim (text), tomato (brand·now·action), leaf (done), mustard (the only caution). v1 had six accents on one screen — green, orange, amber, terracotta, blue, yellow. All gone.

R4The squeeze is the signature

Rail segment length ∝ duration; a squeezed block compresses with a dashed mustard spine + one mono delta. The replan must be legible with zero reading — this animation is the demo video and the differentiator.

R5Teach once, inline, then never

No glossary screen, no teaching framework. First appearance of a concept → one dismissible caption ≤ 90 chars, stored as a seen-flag. "How ketchup works" (S9) is the whole manual.

R6Decoration is deleted

Header = date + avatar. No slogan, no eyebrow, no gradients, no rings, no per-row ⓘ buttons. Personality lives in copy and the squeeze motion, not ornament.

BANNever again (from the v1 screenshots)

  • ⚠ warning triangles — v1 stacked five in a column. A no-guilt app may not use hazard icons. squeeze = dashed mustard spine + delta, nothing else
  • Stat tiles — "0/9 done today" is a guilt counter at 10:40 AM; "Reflowed · engine adjusted" is the system admiring itself. Deleted, not replaced.
  • System jargon on screen — "Reflowed", "engine", "budget", "fixed", "ANCHOR" never appear. "60 m left of 60m budget" → "60 min left"
  • Durations inside labels — "BIG Project Block (2 hrs)" → label "Big project", duration as data. Fix at the Life-JSON level.
  • Redundant numbers — never state the same value twice ("−60m · 60m budget" + "60 m left of 60m budget" on one card).
  • Duplicate drift summaries — the whisper exists exactly once, on the one Today surface.
  • Toasts — confirmations are inline leaf captions.

~Falling behind. When a block runs long, the rest of your day slides. ketchup notices and catches you up.
~The squeeze. Flexible blocks give up minutes first — never below their minimum.
◉Locked. Anchors never move. ketchup plans around them, not through them.
